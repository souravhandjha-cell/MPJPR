Aims & Scope

MPJPR welcomes scientifically sound work across pharmaceutical and allied sciences.

Pharmaceutics

Drug delivery, formulation development, nanotechnology, biopharmaceutics and pharmaceutical technology.

Pharmacology

Experimental pharmacology, toxicology, pharmacological screening and related studies.

Pharmaceutical Chemistry

Medicinal chemistry, analytical chemistry, drug design and characterization.

Pharmacognosy

Natural products, herbal medicines, phytochemistry and related investigations.

Pharmacy Practice

Clinical pharmacy, hospital pharmacy, pharmacovigilance and medication-use research.

Allied Pharmaceutical Sciences

Interdisciplinary research relevant to pharmaceutical and healthcare sciences.
