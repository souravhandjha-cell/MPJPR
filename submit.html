What to Send

  • Complete manuscript (.docx)
  • Cover letter
  • Author declaration/originality statement
  • Ethics approval/consent where applicable
  • Funding and conflict-of-interest statements
  • Tables/figures where required

Submission Checklist

  • Original work and not under simultaneous consideration elsewhere
  • All authors have approved the submission
  • Author and corresponding-author details are complete
  • Abstract, keywords, references, tables and figures are complete
  • Ethics/consent requirements are addressed where applicable

Peer-Review & Editorial Workflow

1. Submission

Manuscript and required documents are received by the editorial office.

2. Initial Screening

Scope, completeness, formatting and basic ethical requirements are checked.

3. Peer Review

Suitable manuscripts are evaluated by independent reviewers.

4. Revision

Authors may be asked to respond to reviewer comments and revise the manuscript.

5. Final Decision

The editorial decision is made based on the review and editorial assessment.

6. Publication

Accepted articles proceed to final preparation and publication.

Submission does not guarantee acceptance. Decisions are based on journal scope, scientific quality, ethics and peer review.
