About the Journal

MPJPR is an international, peer-reviewed, open-access journal focusing on pharmaceutical sciences and related areas of healthcare and biomedical research. The journal aims to promote the exchange of scientific knowledge and encourage high-quality research among researchers, academicians, pharmaceutical scientists, healthcare professionals and students.

Aims & Scope

The primary aim of MPJPR is to provide a credible platform for dissemination of high-quality research and scholarly communication in pharmaceutical sciences and allied disciplines.

Pharmaceutical Sciences

Pharmaceutics, pharmaceutical technology, pharmaceutical chemistry, medicinal chemistry, pharmaceutical analysis and drug delivery.

Biomedical & Clinical

Pharmacology, toxicology, pharmacy practice, clinical pharmacy, pharmacovigilance and allied biomedical sciences.

Emerging Research

Biotechnology, natural products, pharmacokinetics, regulatory affairs, quality systems, computational and AI applications.

Peer-Review & Editorial Workflow

1. Submission

Manuscript is received by the editorial office and acknowledged as appropriate.

2. Initial Screening

Scope, completeness, formatting, originality and basic ethical concerns are checked.

3. Peer Review

Suitable manuscripts are sent for independent peer review, with double-blind review used wherever practicable.

4. Revision

Authors may be asked to respond to reviewer comments and submit a revised manuscript.

5. Final Decision

The editor makes the final publication decision based on the review and editorial assessment.

6. Publication

Accepted manuscripts proceed to editorial preparation, proofreading and publication in the appropriate issue.

Important: Submission does not guarantee acceptance. Editorial decisions are based on journal scope, manuscript quality, ethical compliance and peer-review outcomes.

Journal Policies

Peer Review Policy

MPJPR intends to use a double-blind peer-review process wherever practicable. Manuscripts undergo editorial screening, originality/ethics checks, reviewer assessment, revision where required, final editorial decision and production.

Publication Ethics

The journal does not tolerate plagiarism, duplicate publication, fabrication, falsification, improper authorship, undisclosed conflicts of interest or unethical research practices.

Plagiarism

Submissions may undergo similarity screening. Authors are responsible for originality and proper attribution. Ethical concerns may be investigated before or after publication.

Authorship

All listed authors should have made meaningful contributions and approved the final manuscript. Gift, honorary and guest authorship are not acceptable.

Conflict of Interest & Funding

Authors must disclose relevant financial/non-financial conflicts and all sources of research funding. Where none exists, an appropriate declaration should be provided.

Research Ethics

Research involving human participants, identifiable human data or animals must comply with applicable ethical requirements and provide approval/consent information where applicable.

AI-Assisted Tools

AI tools may be used for appropriate assistance such as language improvement. AI tools cannot be authors. Authors remain responsible for scientific accuracy, originality, data, references and conclusions and should disclose substantial AI use where relevant.

Corrections & Retractions

Corrections, expressions of concern or retractions may be issued when necessary to protect the integrity of the scientific record.

Copyright & Licensing

Copyright and licensing terms will be clearly stated for published articles. Authors must ensure that submitted material does not infringe third-party rights.

Publication Charges

Currently ₹0: MPJPR does not charge a submission fee or Article Processing Charge. Any future change will be communicated clearly before implementation.
